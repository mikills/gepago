# Acme Robotics

Acme Robotics is a Boston-based company that builds warehouse automation systems. Its headquarters are in Boston, Massachusetts.

In 2024, Acme Robotics launched Atlas, an autonomous mobile robot for moving inventory between warehouse stations. Atlas uses the Scout navigation system and was designed by the Acme Robotics engineering team.

Maya Chen is the chief executive officer of Acme Robotics. She announced the Atlas launch at the North American Automation Conference in Chicago. The conference took place in May 2024.

Acme Robotics partnered with Northstar Logistics to pilot Atlas in three distribution centers. Northstar Logistics operates those centers in Illinois and Indiana. The pilot is scheduled to run through December 2025.
